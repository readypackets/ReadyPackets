# Customer Guide

**Applies to:** ReadyPackets Portal
**Audience:** ReadyPackets customers

This guide walks through the portal from creating an account to receiving your deliverables.

## Creating an account

Choose **Sign in** and then **Create an account**. You will need a working email address, because a verification link is sent to it and important notices about your orders go to the same address.

Your password must be at least twelve characters and include upper and lower case letters, a number, and a symbol. The strength indicator updates as you type. Three kinds of password are rejected outright: common passwords from published breach lists, keyboard or numeric sequences, and anything containing your own name or the first part of your email address. That last rule catches the passwords that are easiest for an attacker to guess about you specifically.

After registering, open the verification link in your email. If it has not arrived within a few minutes, check your spam folder and then request a new one from the sign-in page.

### Protecting your account

Enable two-factor authentication under **Security**. You scan a QR code with an authenticator application, and from then on sign-in asks for a six-digit code alongside your password. This is the single most effective step you can take: it means a stolen password alone is not enough to reach your commercial information.

You will be shown ten backup codes at enrolment. Save them somewhere safe and offline. Each works once, and they are how you get in if you lose your phone.

## The dashboard

Your dashboard opens with a short checklist covering your profile, the mutual non-disclosure agreement, your first packet selection, and your intake questionnaire. It disappears once complete.

Below that you will find your active orders with their current phase, any deliverables ready to download, any questions our team has asked you, and your recent activity.

## The mutual non-disclosure agreement

Before intake, you will be asked to review and accept the mutual NDA. It protects both directions: what you tell us about your business, and what we share with you in the course of the work.

Read it in the portal, then accept it. Your acceptance is recorded with a timestamp and the exact version of the document you agreed to, and you can retrieve that version at any time from your account, even after we publish a newer one.

## Placing an order

Open **Packets** to see the catalogue. Each packet group addresses a distinct area, and most groups offer more than one tier so you can match depth to need. Every packet page sets out what is included and what you will receive.

Select the packets you want in **New order**. The quote updates as you go, itemising each line and showing the total.

> **The All-In bundle discount.** When your selection spans six or more distinct packet groups, a fifteen percent discount is applied to the subtotal automatically. You do not need a code, and the configurator tells you how many more groups would qualify you.

Some packets, including institutional engagements, are quoted individually rather than priced publicly. These appear with an enquiry route instead of a price, and our team will respond with a scoped quotation.

Review the summary and submit. Your order is created immediately and appears on your dashboard.

## Phase I intake

This is the part that determines the quality of everything that follows. The questionnaire gathers the specifics of your business and your objectives.

Your answers save automatically as you work, so you can close the tab and return later without losing anything. Where a question does not apply, say so rather than leaving it blank: a stated "not applicable" is information, whereas a blank is ambiguous and usually results in us asking you about it later.

You can attach supporting documents where the form invites them. Accepted formats include PDF, common image formats, Office documents, plain text, CSV, and audio. A file is checked against its actual contents rather than its name, so if a document has been renamed at some point it may be refused; re-saving it in its proper format resolves this.

Submit when complete. You will not be able to edit afterwards, but our team can ask follow-up questions through the order, and you can answer them there.

## Tracking your order

Every order shows its phase.

| Phase | What is happening |
| --- | --- |
| New | Created; intake not yet started |
| Phase I — Intake | You are completing the questionnaire |
| Phase II — Synthesis | We are analysing your responses |
| In production | Your deliverables are being produced |
| Delivered | Your deliverables are available to download |
| Closed | Complete |

Alongside the phase you will see a completion percentage, the expected date where one has been set, notes from our team, and any outstanding questions. Answering a question promptly is the most reliable way to keep the expected date accurate.

## Receiving your deliverables

Deliverables appear under **Files**, grouped by order, and you are notified by email when new ones are released.

Each download is authorised individually at the moment you request it, which is why a download link cannot be forwarded or reused — it is issued for you, for that one transfer. If a link does not work, return to the portal and start the download again.

Where a deliverable has been revised, the newer version is shown along with the history, so you can see what changed and when.

## Support

Open a ticket under **Support** for anything relating to an order or your account. Choose the category that fits, describe the issue, and attach anything relevant. You will be notified when we reply, and the full conversation stays with the ticket.

If a ticket is closed and the matter is not resolved, reopen it rather than starting a new one. Keeping the history together means you do not have to explain the background again.

## Community

The community area holds discussion topics and customer reviews. Reviews are moderated before publication, which keeps the section useful rather than adopted by spam.

When posting, remember it is a shared space: do not include confidential details of your own business or anyone else's.

## Your account and your data

Under **Profile** you can update your details and choose which notifications you receive. Under **Security** you can change your password, manage two-factor authentication, and review every active session — each with its location and device, with your current session marked. If you see a session you do not recognise, revoke it and change your password.

Changing your password signs out every other session automatically.

Two rights are available directly from your account settings without needing to ask.

> **Export your data.** Download everything we hold about you: profile, orders, intake responses, support history, and the details of your files.

> **Request erasure.** Ask for your personal data to be deleted. We will confirm the request, explain anything we are legally required to retain such as financial records, and complete the rest.

## Common questions

**I have not received the verification email.** Check your spam folder first, then request a new link from the sign-in page. If your organisation filters external mail aggressively, ask your IT team to allow our sending domain.

**I have lost my authenticator.** Use one of your backup codes to sign in, then re-enrol a new device from Security. If your backup codes are also gone, contact support; identity verification is required before we can reset the factor.

**My file upload was refused.** The contents are checked against the declared format, so a file that was renamed at some point can be rejected. Re-save it in its correct format. Also confirm it is within the size limit shown on the form.

**Everything is returning an error after several attempts.** Repeated rapid attempts trigger a temporary protective limit. Wait a few minutes and try again.

**I have been signed out unexpectedly.** Sessions expire after a period of inactivity and after a maximum duration, both for your protection. You will be warned shortly before expiry and offered the chance to continue.

**My order shows no expected date.** A date is set once the work is scoped. If you need an estimate sooner, ask on the order or open a ticket.
