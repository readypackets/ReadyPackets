## REFUND POLICY

Ready Packets Consulting Last Updated: 8/19/2026

At ReadyPackets Consulting, we stand behind the quality of our work. This Refund Policy outlines the circumstances under which refunds may be issued. We strive to be fair and transparent in all refund decisions.

## 1. CANCELLATION BEFORE WORK BEGINS

If you cancel your order before any work has commenced, you are entitled to a full refund minus a 5% administrative processing fee. Cancellation requests must be submitted in writing via email to cancellations@readypackets.com with an acknowledgement message or through the customer portal messaging system.

“Work commenced” means that a team member has been assigned to your project and has begun research, drafting, or other substantive work on your packet.

## 2. CANCELLATION AFTER WORK HAS BEGUN

## 3. AFTER DELIVERY

Once the final deliverable has been made available for download in your portal or sent to your email, the order is considered complete. No refunds are issued for completed deliverables.

However, see our Quality Guarantee (Section 5) if you believe the deliverable does not meet the agreed scope.

## 4. THE ALL IN BUNDLE

For the Concept to Commerce Bundle (ALL in Bundle), refunds are calculated on a per-phase basis:

Phases not yet started: Full refund for those phases

Phase currently in progress: Partial refund per the table above

Phases already delivered: No refund

The 15% bundle discount is recalculated when phases are cancelled. If cancellation reduces the order to fewer than 6 packets, the remaining packets will be billed at their individual (non-discounted) rates.

## 5. QUALITY GUARANTEE

If your delivered packet materially fails to meet the agreed project scope as described in your order confirmation and intake documentation, you have two options:

Revision: We will revise the deliverables at no additional cost to bring it in line with the agreed scope. This is in addition to the standard revision round included with each tier.

Partial Refund: If we are unable to resolve the issue through revision, we will issue a partial refund proportional to the deficient portion of the deliverable.

Quality claims must be submitted within 7 days of delivery with a detailed description of how the deliverable fails to meet the agreed scope. Subjective dissatisfaction (e.g., “I don’t like the tone”) is addressed through the included revision round, not through refunds.

## 6. NON-REFUNDABLE ITEMS

The following items and fees are non-refundable:

• Consultation fees (if applicable) once the consultation has taken place

• Rush or expedited delivery surcharges once expedited work has begun

• Third-party costs incurred on your behalf (e.g., filing fees, domain registrations, software licenses)

• Custom add-ons that have been fully delivered

## 7. HOW TO REQUEST A REFUND

To request a refund, contact us through one of the following methods:

Email: cancellations@readypackets.com with the subject line “Refund Request — [Order Number]”

Portal: Send a message through the customer portal messaging system

Include your order number, the reason for the refund request, and any supporting documentation. We will acknowledge your request within 1 business day and provide a decision within 5 business days.

## 8. REFUND PROCESSING

Approved refunds will be processed to the original payment method:

Credit/debit card refunds: 5–10 business days to appear on your statement

Bank transfer refunds: 3–10 business days

You will receive email confirmation when the refund has been processed.

## 9. DISPUTES

If you disagree with a refund decision, you may escalate the matter to our management team by emailing disputes@readypackets.com with the subject line “Refund Escalation.” We will review the matter and respond within 10 business days. If the dispute cannot be resolved, it will be subject to the dispute resolution provisions in our Terms of Service.

## 10. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We reserve the right to modify this Refund Policy at any time. Changes apply to orders placed after the effective date of the modification. The refund terms in effect at the time of your order placement will govern that order.
