# ReadyPackets Customer Portal Guide

**Version:** 2026-08-12
**Audience:** ReadyPackets customers and workspace collaborators

## Getting started

Open the customer portal using the link supplied by ReadyPackets. You can sign in with your password and MFA, a configured SAML provider, or a customer magic link. Magic links are single-use, expire after 15 minutes, and still require MFA challenge or enrolment before portal access is granted.

The visible **System**, **Light**, and **Dark** controls set the portal appearance. System is the default and follows your device preference.

## Portal areas

| Area | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | View active orders, progress, support replies, announcements, referrals, and links to the related details. |
| **My orders** | Create and review orders, use the persistent packet cart, apply eligible coupons, and proceed to payment when online payments are enabled. |
| **My Business Packets** | Access approved final files and delivered packet material. |
| **Packet Collective** | Create or join organization workspaces, manage collaboration roles, and share selected orders with scoped access. |
| **Support and Community** | Open a support ticket, read replies, and participate in approved community areas. |
| **Knowledge base** | Read customer help articles approved and published by ReadyPackets administrators. |
| **Referrals** | Copy your referral code, use the referral link, send an email sharing link, and review eligible referral statistics. |
| **Policies and Settings** | Review required policies, manage profile details, see the opaque account reference, configure MFA, and manage security sessions. |

## Order intake and questions

ReadyPackets sends order-specific questions from the Order Question Banks. Questions may be assigned to Phase 1, Phase 2, both phases, or no phase. Complete only questions that appear on your order, then upload permitted supporting documents and record the Business Pitch Idea directly in the portal when that feature is enabled for the order.

Do not upload credentials, payment card data, or secrets unless ReadyPackets explicitly requests a secure method for that material. The site does not accept customer audio-file uploads; Business Pitch recordings are recorded directly in the web experience.

## Collaboration roles

Order owners can share an order directly or through Packet Collective. Permissions can allow viewing, document upload, final-deliverable viewing, business-pitch recording, contributor work, or manager-level collaboration. Workspace owners should remove access promptly when a collaborator leaves the organization.

## Payments and coupons

When ReadyPackets has enabled online payments, eligible orders redirect to hosted Stripe Checkout. The portal never collects payment card numbers itself. Coupon methods can provide a percentage off, a fixed amount off, or a defined fixed final cart price. A zero-priced order may complete without asking for card details.

## Security and support

Keep MFA enabled, do not forward magic links, and use Sign out on shared computers. If a required policy is updated, accept it before continuing. Contact support through the portal when you suspect account compromise, unexpected collaboration access, or an incorrect order status.
